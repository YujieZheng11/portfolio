A NOTE FROM THE DESIGNER2022 — 2026

Design begins
with listening.

I’m Yujie Zheng, a graphic designer with a bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design from the University of the Arts London and a master’s degree in Communication Design from the University of Southampton.

This portfolio brings together work made between 2022 and 2026, spanning photography, motion graphics, typography, editorial design and poster art. People are at the heart of my practice, with a particular focus on women’s narratives, community life and emotional experience.

I see design as more than a way to solve problems: it is a way of listening to people, places and feelings. When emotions are overlooked or left unspoken, I use visual language to tell stories, give inner experiences a voice, and create moments of reflection and connection.

Yujie Zheng / Graphic designer

YUJIE ZHENG
GRAPHIC & DIGITAL DESIGN

I’m interested in what
we tend to overlook.

Emotions that don’t have a shape. Letters worn away by time. The quiet relationship between people and technology. My work starts with these small observations, and gives them a visual language.

Across typography, publications, digital interfaces and material experiments, I use research and making to look at familiar things a little differently.

从日常观察出发，用视觉语言表达情绪、记忆，以及人与环境之间不易察觉的联系。
